Our neighbourhood

Character streets, a village high street, and parks at every turn.

Mount Lawley is known for its Federation and inter-war homes, tree-lined avenues and the cafés, bookshops and restaurants of Beaufort Street. We’ve been part of that high street since 1980.

Our listings reach across the surrounding suburbs too, from the character homes of Menora and Coolbinia to North Perth, Maylands, Bayswater and the city fringe.
